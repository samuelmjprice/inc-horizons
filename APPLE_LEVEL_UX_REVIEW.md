# Apple-Level UX Review

Audit date: 1 June 2026

## Observations

- The brand tone is calm, premium, and Mediterranean.
- The official logo treatment is consistent and centered in the hero.
- The main risk is information density, not visual quality.
- The site succeeds when it shows summary first and detail only when expanded.
- Admin/developer sections should stay separate from the onsite operations flow.

## Fixes Made

- Replaced weak long-page navigation with an Apple-like floating section pill.
- Added section-end previous/top/next controls.
- Kept the navigation subtle, rounded, glassy, and low-noise.
- Kept admin-heavy tooling behind Admin Data.
- Preserved collapsed/summary-first behavior across long operational sections.

## Future Recommendations

- Add a compact “Event Mode” toggle later if the team wants to hide all non-critical sections onsite.
- Consider a simple “Today only” mobile home once final data is locked.
- Keep button language short and action-led.
- Avoid adding more visible tags unless they directly help decisions under pressure.
